    Dataset licence review — the 41 that need a decision

    Status: open. Awaiting maintainer decisions. Generated 2026-08-19 from a 92-agent research pass over 46 unlabelled datasets.

    Each dataset was researched by its own agent (licence on the data, not the article; upstream provenance named explicitly) and then assessed against the policy in .claude/skills/dataset-extraction/references/license.md. Three were clear enough to apply automatically. Two more were already decided and are recorded below. The remaining 41 need a person, but they are not 41 separate questions - they collapse into ten families, and four of those families account for 17 datasets.

    Nothing in this file has been applied. No script was edited for any dataset listed here.

    How to work through this

    The families are ordered roughly easiest-first. A decision on one family applies to every dataset in it, so the four upstream families at the top (ZINC, GDB, EPI Suite, PubChem) settle 17 datasets between them.

    For each family the question is the same one you answered for ChEMBL: does the upstream's licence permit redistribution, and if it carries an obligation, is the project willing to pass that obligation on? The machinery to record the answer already exists - the license field, ADR 009, and the Settled section of rejected_datasets.md.

    A caution about the research. Confidence is self-reported by the agent that did the work. Where it says low, treat the finding as a starting point rather than a conclusion: in several cases the agent could not reach the source at all, and "no licence found" then means "not found by this agent" rather than "none exists". The high-confidence rows quote licence text they actually read.
